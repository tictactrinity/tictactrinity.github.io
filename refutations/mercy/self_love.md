# [Refutations](/refutations/) > [Mercy](/refutations/mercy/) > Self Loving God

Author: Fasih Rana  
Dated: 27-September-2026

## Introduction
Trinitarians claim that God is eternally _love_ therefore he must have always had an object of love. The father loves the Son perfectly and eternally and therefore does not require love from his creation. Is this God loving another or is this self love?

## Premises
**P1**. Love requires an object genuinely other than the one that loves.  
**P2**. For two things to have a real relationship, they have to be separate things before and independent of that relationship, you can't become separate just by relating.  
**P3**. Trinitarian doctrine says the Father and Son are the same one being (along with the HS), and are only distinguished by their relationship (begetting/begotten), not separate beings prior to or apart from that relationship.  
**P4**. By P2, this means Father and Son aren't really separate, the relationship that's supposed to distinguish them can't do that job, since something has to already be separate for a relationship to hold between them in the first place.

## Conclusion
Therefore either the Father and Son are truly separate beings (denying P3, and abandoning monotheism), or they are not truly separate (as P3 states), in which case the "relationship" between them isn't a real relationship between two things, and the Father's love for the Son is just God relating to and loving himself which fails P1, the same standard used to disqualify a unitary God.
