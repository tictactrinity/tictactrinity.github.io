# [Refutations](/refutations/) > [Mercy](/refutations/mercy/) > Love Contradiction

Author: Fasih Rana  
Dated: 26-September-2026

## Introduction
Christians who believe in substitution atonement present [Romans 5:8](https://biblehub.com/romans/5-8.htm) as evidence of "Jesus dying for humanity's sins".

## Premises
**P1**. The Trinitarian God is all love  
**P2**. Love, by nature, wills the good of the one loved.  
**P3**. As  per [Romans 5:8](https://biblehub.com/romans/5-8.htm), God's love is demonstrated through the Son's death, suffering the penalty for sin committed by others against God.  
**P4**. Suffering death at the hands of, and for the sin of, a third party does not will the good of the one who suffers.

## Conclusion
Therefore this act fails what love-toward-its-object requires (P2 + P4), contradicting P1's claim that God is love without remainder. Since sacrificing your son for a sin committed by someone else against you is not an act of love toward the son, the Trinitarian God cannot be the perfect expression of love as Trinitarians propose.
