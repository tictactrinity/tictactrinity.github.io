# [Refutations](/refutations/) > [Mercy](/refutations/mercy/) > Does God Need Love?

Author: Fasih Rana  
Dated: 26-September-2026

## Introduction
Christians claim God needs no love. But is it true?

## Premises
**P1**. The Father eternally, perfectly loves the Son.  
**P2**. God needs nothing, specifically love, from creation.  
**P3**. God creates the world anyway, though unneeded.  
**P4**. God gives up the beloved, only-begotten Son for the world ([John 3:16](https://biblehub.com/john/3-16.htm); [Romans 5:8](https://biblehub.com/romans/5-8.htm)).  
**P5**. Giving up something you already have in perfect supply, love, for something you didn't need, is only rational if you love that second thing at least as much.

## Conclusion
So either God did need the world after all (denying P2), or God loved the world as much as, or more than, the Son he calls his "only begotten"; making that love imperfect, since it was not uniquely his (denying P1).
