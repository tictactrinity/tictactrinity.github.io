# [Refutations](/refutations/) > [Mercy](/refutations/mercy_and_love/) > Imitation of Mercy

Author: Fasih Rana  
Dated: 26-September-2026

## Introduction
In the Gospel of Luke Jesus leaves a way to show mercy. This is a test of how people need to show mercy to transgressors against themselves.

## Premises
**P1**. [Luke 6:36](https://biblehub.com/luke/6-36.htm) commands the adherent to imitate the Father's mercy: "Be merciful, just as your Father is merciful."  
**P2**. [John 14:6](https://biblehub.com/john/14-6.htm) Jesus says "I am the way, the truth, and the life," establishing his own act as the pattern of life.  
**P3**. [Romans 5:8](https://biblehub.com/romans/5-8.htm) God's love is demonstrated through the Son's death, suffering the penalty for sin committed by others against God.  
**P4**. To imitate this mercy per P1 and P2 is to replicate the mechanism itself.

## Conclusion
Therefore, a follower of Jesus who believes P2 must be prepared to offer himself, or his own son, to be sacrificed for a sin committed against himself or his father.
