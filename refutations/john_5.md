# [Refutations](/refutations/) > John 5 - Does Jesus Judge on his own?

**Author: Fasih Rana, Date: 19-Aug-2026**

# Intro

John 5 is often presented as a (partial) proof for Jesus's and one of the arguments put forth is that Jesus is the sole Judge. [John 5:22](https://biblehub.com/john/5-22.htm) is one of the most cited verses for it.

> For the Father judges no one, but has committed all judgment to the Son,

This, Christians say, is the proof that Jesus is _God_ and he will come back to judge all of humanity, but lets test this.

# Jesus is Dependent
## Premises

**P1.** Jesus always tells the truth.  
**P2.** Jesus says in 5:19, the Son can do nothing of himself, but only what he sees the Father doing.  
**P3.** Jesus says in 5:22, the Father judges no one.  
**P4.** Jesus says in 5:30, the Son judges as he hears.  

## Conclusions

**C1.** If P3 means the Father is entirely uninvolved in judgment, then P4 is false — you cannot hear guidance from someone with no involvement.  
**C2.** If C1 is true, P2 is also false. The Son would be acting entirely independently, doing something of himself.  
**C3.** If C1 is true, Jesus contradicts himself three times within the same passage.  
**C4.** If C3 is true, P1 is false which would mean Jesus is a liar.  

# Jesus is not Almighty
These premises were formulated based on the debate of [Shiekh Uthman vs 10 Christian Apologists](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HMfA-FtYWMI&t=3825s). The initial prompt was **Jesus is not Almighty God according to the Bible**. Shiekh Uthman is talking to Jerome Chukwuwem, who initialliy responds by asking _"if Jesus claimed to be almighty, would he then be Almighty God?"_. Later on Jerome makes a statement that _"the author of John believes that Jesus is equal to the Father"_. This goes away from the initial prompt but lets formulate some premises anyway.

## Premises
**P1.** If Jesus is Almighty, then Jesus is Almighty God.  
**P2.** If Jesus is Almighty God, then Jesus possesses a supreme will.  
**P3.** A will that is ultimately determined by another person's will is not supreme.  
**P4.** Jesus says that he does nothing from himself and judges according to what he hears from the Father.

## Conclusions
**C1.** Therefore, Jesus' judgment is ultimately determined by the Father's will rather than by Jesus' own will.  
**C2.** Therefore, Jesus' will is not supreme with respect to judgment.  
**C3.** Therefore, Jesus does not satisfy the condition required for being Almighty God.  
**C4.** Therefore, Jesus is not Almighty God.  
**C5.** Therefore, Jesus is not Almighty.
