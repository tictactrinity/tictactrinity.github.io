# [Refutations](/refutations/) > [John 10:30](/refutations/john_10_30/) >  Does Jesus Claim Ontological Divinity?

Author: Fasih Rana  
Date: 11-Aug-2026  

## Argumnent

P1. Jesus always tells the truth.
P2. In John 10:30 Jesus claims ontological divinity.
P3. Jesus does not dispute the charge in John 10:33 that He made Himself equal to God, making the accusation legitimate by His own silence.
P4. Jesus rather than correcting them, affirms "god" as a scripturally valid and uncorrected designator in John 10:34.

## Conclusions

C1. If Jesus is ontologically God per trinitarian claims, and affirms "god" as a legitimate designator applying to others, then ontological divinity is not exclusive to Jesus.
C2. Trinitarianism therefore cannot coherently maintain monotheism — as it affirms multiple real referents of the designator "god" — which would either be henotheism or polytheism.
